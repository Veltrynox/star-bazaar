## 1. Bot changes (qa/bots/bot_run.gd only)

1. Buy-order guard (`_buy_wanted`): the Buy order is on when cash covers one trip, meaning `fixed_cost()` (trip plus upkeep of one run). Reserve chosen: none beyond that. The engine's `route_reserve` (game/route_engine.gd ~683) already holds the fixed cost of every running route before a Buy spends, so the old extra `+ one unit` term double-counted it and blocked the Buy at cash 114 (fixed 110).
2. Plate sell: `_apply_orders` sets a Sell order for plate at the plate-selling planet (found by its `plate_sell` price, index 0 = Voltara) while the system storage holds plate. Helper `_plate_seller()`. First attempt used index 1 and was refused ("Plate is not traded here"); corrected, no order failures in the final batches.
3. A1 before the permit (permit 2 is the only one used): `_buy_wanted_permit` waits while A1 is not learned (unless A1 is skipped or excluded by `--skills`). `_learn_skills` exempts A1 from the permit points reserve so A1 can be bought first.

Not changed: game code, data, balance, tests, GDD. Nothing staged or committed.
