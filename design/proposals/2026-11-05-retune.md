# Retune v49: decent regression and CEO x ship fairness (2026-11-05)

Economy designer. Applied to `data/balance.json` (v48 to v49) and `data/ships.json`. Not committed.

## 1. Why decent fell from 3/4 to 1/4
Bisect, decent, `--skip-tutorial`, seeds 1-4, HEAD data against the working tree.
- HEAD `goods.json` alone or HEAD `skills.json` alone: no change (1/4).
- HEAD `balance.json` + `skills.json` together: 3/4 (6/8 on seeds 1-8). So the loss comes from the balance keys the new skills no longer read.
- Restoring single removed keys on the working tree: `skill_MIL_3_route_units` 0.125 gives 4/4; `skill_LUX_1_price` 0.06 helps seed 2 only; `skill_size_s/m`, `skill_MIL_4_price`, `skill_BIO_1_order_units`: no change.
- Cause: MIL_3 changed from +12.5 pct mil route capacity to debris immunity. The default bot learns MIL_3 in every run, so it lost 12.5 pct volume on its mil lanes. Share needs volume.
- Not causes: recipe cost changes (armour, gems), MIL_6 0.35, MIL_5 0.35/4, CEO and ship defaults (default start has no CEO and the small ship).

Perk and stat design untouched; ceos.json and other ship rows unchanged. The man_02 reveal perk still feeds no share (first cycle only); haggling carries that CEO via cash and earlier ships.

## 3. Results (decent, `--skip-tutorial`)
Default start (no CEO, small ship), seeds 1-8: 6 wins (c27 all), 2 END_OUTCOMPETED. Seeds 1-4: 3/4.

Regression, seeds 1-4: decent 3 wins; sloppy 0/4 (cash 8.5k-10k, 1 ship); always_expand 0/4 (24k-31k, 6 ships); never_expand 0/4 (5.8k-9.5k, 2-3 ships). All losses END_OUTCOMPETED c40. No dominant strategy.

16 combos, seeds 1-6 (96 runs): 72 wins (75 pct), was 34 (35 pct). Median win cycle 27 in 15 combos, 30 in one (man_02 dart, 3 wins).
- By CEO: man_01 18/24, man_02 19, woman_01 18, woman_02 17.
- By ship: small 17/24, bulk 18, dart 17, fleet 19.
- Pass rule (median within 1, win rate at least 60 pct of best = 4 of 6): 13 of 16 combos pass both. Misses: man_01 small 3/6, woman_01 small 3/6, man_02 dart 3/6 (median 30). Aggregates by CEO and ship are within 20 pct of each other; per-combo n=6 is noisy (one run moves a combo across the line), and best=6 sets a high bar.
Scouting runs (about 250 more) tried cargo 34-44, maintenance 30-34, order_amount 44, haggling 0.014 and 0.02; these are not in the table.

## 4. Caveats
- Win cycle is pinned near 27 by win_earliest_cycle 23 plus 5 hold cycles; only the win rate moves. Win in the 22-26 skilled band needs a human.
- The sweep ran before the mining_yield change; the 20-run regression was re-run after it with identical results. Decent does not depend on cluster ore.
- cargo 40 also raises every non-decent strategy's volume; they still lose by a wide margin.
- Bot only. tools/sim.py does not model CEO or skills.
