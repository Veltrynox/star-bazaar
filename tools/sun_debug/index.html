Sun debug: one tile per grid plane

Each tile shows one plane of the sun alone, as game/fx/sun/sun.gdshader samples it: the plane's own sheet (storm, flare or calm; one sheet per plane, repeats allowed), its crop centre, crop scale and crop rotation on the cut frame, a round soft edge (smoothstep 0.55..1.0), the plane's frame phase with linear blending of frame f and f+1, times glow, added onto black. The combined view adds all planes with additive blending. This is a 2D approximation of the 3D placement: yaw, pitch and the depth offset are listed but not drawn (every plane is shown face-on and centred), and the slow spin of each plane is only drawn if you move the spin slider. The numbers come from ; per-plane values (phase, crop, rotation, yaw/pitch, spin) are not stored there, they are computed in _ready()/_layout(), so make_planes.py repeats those formulas and writes planes.js. Yaw and pitch are derived from the plane direction for display. Regenerate with python3 tools/sun_debug/make_planes.py.
